Pricing Strategies

The agent employs multiple proven pricing strategies tailored to your needs

💵 Cost-Plus Pricing

Adds appropriate markup to your costs

  • B2C: 30-50% markup
  • B2B: 15-30% markup
Best for: Commodity products, manufacturing, retail

💎 Value-Based Pricing

Prices based on customer perceived value

  • Best for unique products
  • Premium pricing for differentiation
Best for: Unique products, professional services

🎯 Competitive Pricing

Positions price relative to competitors

  • Market-aware pricing
  • 90-110% of competitor prices
Best for: Crowded markets, similar offerings

🚀 Penetration Pricing

Lower prices to gain market share

  • Ideal for new market entry
  • 60-85% of market price
Best for: New market entry, building customer base

⭐ Price Skimming

Premium pricing for innovative products

  • First-to-market advantage
  • 130-250% premium pricing
Best for: Innovative products, limited competition

🔄 Subscription Pricing

Recurring revenue models

  • Based on customer LTV
  • 5-15% of LTV per month
Best for: SaaS products, membership programs
